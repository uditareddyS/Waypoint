# MSME Scheme Knowledge Base

## Scheme: PM SVANidhi

**What it is:** A central government micro-credit scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs to give street vendors small, collateral-free working capital loans. Restructured in August 2025 and extended till 31 March 2030, so new vendors can still apply.

**Loan structure (ladder system):**
- First loan: up to ₹15,000, repayable in about 12 months
- Second loan: up to ₹25,000, available after repaying the first
- Third loan: up to ₹50,000
- No collateral required at any stage

**Extra benefits:**
- 7% per annum interest subsidy for on-time/early repayment, credited quarterly
- No penalty for early repayment
- Cashback for digital transactions
- No processing fee
- Loan can be used for any business purpose (raw materials, rent, equipment)

**Who's eligible:** Street vendors with a Certificate of Vending / Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), or vendors identified in the survey who haven't been issued one yet. Covers vendors selling goods (vegetables, fruits, food, tea, textiles, footwear) and services (barbershops, cobblers, laundry).

**How to apply:** Official portal pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in, a nearby Common Service Centre (CSC), or a participating bank.

## Scheme: PMEGP (Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme)

**What it is:** A government credit-linked subsidy scheme implemented by the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) to help set up new micro-enterprises and generate employment.

**How the funding works:**
- Beneficiaries get a subsidy of 15–35% of the project cost from the government
- General category: 15% subsidy (urban), 25% (rural)
- Special category (SC/ST/OBC/minority/ex-servicemen/PH): 25% subsidy (urban), 35% (rural)
- Remaining amount comes as a bank term loan
- Interest rates typically range 11–12%, varying by bank

**Project cost limits:**
- Manufacturing enterprises: up to ₹50 lakh
- Service/business enterprises: up to ₹20 lakh

**Who's eligible:**
- Any Indian citizen aged 18+
- For projects above ₹10 lakh (manufacturing) or ₹5 lakh (services), applicant must have passed 8th standard
- Only NEW projects — not available to existing businesses already availing a subsidy under another government scheme
- Self-help groups, charitable trusts, and registered societies can also apply

**How to apply:** Through the official KVIC/PMEGP e-portal or a nearby bank branch. Requires: application form, project report, ID/address proof, PAN, Aadhaar, and 8th-pass certificate if applicable.

## Scheme: Mudra Loan (Shishu Category)

**What it is:** The entry-level category under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY), a collateral-free loan scheme designed specifically for early-stage micro enterprises — a strong fit for street vendors and first-time borrowers.

**Loan categories under PMMY:**
- Shishu: up to ₹50,000 (best fit for street vendors)
- Kishor: ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh
- Tarun: ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh

**Key features (Shishu):**
- Fully collateral-free, no guarantor needed
- Minimal documentation — basic KYC, proof of business activity, bank details
- Interest rates typically 7.3–12% per annum depending on lender (no fixed government rate)
- Flexible repayment tenure, usually up to 5 years
- Borrowers may receive a Mudra RuPay card for working capital access

**Who's eligible:**
- Indian citizens aged 18+
- New or existing micro-enterprise owners in manufacturing, trading, or services (non-farm)
- Includes sole proprietors, shopkeepers, street vendors, artisans, service providers
- First-time borrowers welcome

**How to apply:** Through udyamimitra.in or directly at a partner bank/NBFC/microfinance institution.

## Scheme: NULM / DAY-NULM (National Urban Livelihoods Mission)

**What it is:** A central scheme by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs aimed at reducing urban poverty by helping the urban poor — including street vendors — access self-employment, skill training, and credit.

**Financial benefits:**
- Individual micro-enterprise loans: up to ₹2 lakh at 7% interest (some sources cite up to ₹4 lakh under DAY-NULM 2.0 at 5% subsidized interest)
- Group enterprise loans: up to ₹10 lakh
- Self-help groups (SHGs) get loans at 7% interest; women's SHGs repaying on time get an extra 3% subvention, effectively 4%
- Skill development support of ₹15,000–18,000 per urban poor beneficiary

**Non-financial support for street vendors specifically:**
- Helps with development of vending zones and vendor markets
- Civic infrastructure support: road paving, water supply, storage spaces, lighting, waste disposal
- Helps street vendors get access to suitable vending spaces and social security

**Who's eligible:** Urban poor, homeless individuals, and street vendors in India's statutory towns/cities.

**How to apply:** Through the local Urban Local Body (ULB) or the NULM state mission office; often coordinated through City Livelihood Centres.
