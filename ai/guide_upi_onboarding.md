## Guide: UPI/QR Merchant Onboarding (Paytm/PhonePe/BharatPe)

**Why it matters:** Street vendors without digital payment acceptance lose sales to customers who don't carry cash. Setting up a UPI QR code is free, takes minutes, and needs no POS hardware.

**General steps (works similarly across PhonePe, Paytm, BharatPe):**
1. Install the business/merchant app (e.g. "PhonePe Business", "Paytm for Business", "BharatPe").
2. Sign up with your mobile number (must be linked to your bank account).
3. Enter the OTP sent to your phone to verify.
4. Complete basic KYC — this usually needs:
   - Aadhaar card
   - PAN card
   - A bank account in your name (or linked mobile number)
5. Enter your shop/business details — name, category, and location (helps customers find you nearby).
6. Once verified, the app auto-generates your personal merchant QR code.
7. Download the QR code image and print it, or save it to show digitally at your stall.
8. Display the QR code prominently at your stall so customers can scan and pay.

**Static vs Dynamic QR:**
- **Static QR** — one fixed code, customer types in the amount manually. Best for street vendors and small shops with variable pricing (e.g., different fruit weights each sale).
- **Dynamic QR** — generates a new code per transaction with the amount pre-filled. Useful for fixed-price items but needs more setup — not necessary for most street vendors starting out.

**No cost to set up:** No processing fee, no minimum balance, no hardware required — just a smartphone.

**After setup:** Encourage customers to scan and pay; most schemes and reports show that consistent digital transaction history helps build credit eligibility for larger loans later (e.g. under PM SVANidhi's ladder system).
